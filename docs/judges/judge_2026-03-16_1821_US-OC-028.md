### Design Concerns
1. **[Minor] Cross-import for group_step_output** — agent_loop.py imports `from ..openclaw_agent import group_step_output`. This creates a circular-ish dependency (agent_loop.py → openclaw_agent.py). Moving `group_step_output` and `_find_latest_screenshot` into an openclaw/ submodule (e.g. `openclaw/transcript.py`) would complete the separation. *(Design Soundness)*
2. **[Info] state.json compaction_count consistency** — state.json showed compaction_count=0 during mid-run read but 1 after completion. This is expected (file updated asynchronously), but a flush-on-read guard could prevent stale reads during debugging. *(Implementation Progress)*
3. **[Info] tokensBefore=6320 on 50k window** — Compaction triggered with only 6320 tokens before, suggesting the token estimation or threshold formula may be aggressive for small context windows. Not a bug (testing override), but worth noting. *(Real-World Behavior)*
